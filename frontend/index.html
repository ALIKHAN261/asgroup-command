Admin power, with guardrails.

You gave the team admin access to your PC. Here's what keeps it safe:

🖱

Propose → tap → execute

Agents can't click anything on their own. Every mouse/keyboard action arrives as a proposal with a screenshot — it runs only on your tap.

🚫

Hard blocks

Passwords, OTPs, 2FA, CAPTCHAs, logins — agents can never drive these. Ever. Even if allowlisted.

🗣

Voice can't approve

The voice assistant talks and informs, but a spoken "yes" never counts. Approvals need your tap in the dashboard.

🛑

Panic corner

Slam the mouse into any screen corner and any control action aborts instantly. One crashing agent never stops the others.

Questions.

Does it work from my phone?

Yes. The dashboard is a website — open it on your phone, enter your backend address and token once. From anywhere, use Tailscale (free) to reach your PC privately.

Windows only?

The backend (where agents live) runs on Windows — that's where your work is. The dashboard works on any device with a browser.

Can the agents really control my PC?

Yes — with your explicit grant. They propose each action with a screenshot; you tap "Approve & run". You can also allowlist safe actions (like screenshots) to run without asking.

What can't it do?

Agents never send messages, post, publish, or spend on their own. They never touch logins, OTPs, or CAPTCHAs. Voice informs but never approves.

Is it free?

Yes — it's your software on your PC. No subscriptions, no cloud fees. The dashboard hosting is on Vercel's free tier.
